## Split rule (declared values; not changed after any real-plant number)
Per compound leaf with ≥ 100 labelled surfels and a rachis curve (others keep the `_02` helper leaflets, flag `split_not_possible_kept_helpers`):
1. **Frame.** Rachis curve resampled at 0.25 mm; extended beyond its tip along the last 2 mm direction by 60 mm (so s > L is possible) and before its base by 0 mm. Each surfel → nearest point → arclength s (0 at the node), distance r to the curve, side = sign((t × **up**) · (p − foot)), **up = +z** (board frame); t × up of length < 0.05 → side from the previous valid sample.
2. **Rachis surfels:** r ≤ **1.0 mm**. Their area share is reported.
3. **Terminal.** Histogram of s of all non-rachis surfels, bin **1 mm**, smoothed with a Gaussian of FWHM **2 mm**; gap = maximal run of bins whose smoothed count ≤ **2 %** of the maximum bin. s_T = start of the **last gap whose start lies in (0.5 L, s_max]** (L = curve length to the last click, s_max = largest surfel s); if none, **s_T = 0.8 L** (flag `terminal_no_gap`). Terminal = non-rachis surfels with s ≥ s_T.
4. **Laterals.** Per side, non-rachis surfels with s < s_T; a side with < **30** surfels → m = 0. Otherwise 1-D Gaussian mixture on s (sklearn, full, `random_state=0`, `n_init=5`) for m ∈ {1, 2, 3}; BIC each; **data-supported** = BIC ≤ BIC_min + **10**. Prior per side: n = spec leaflets, pairs p = (n − 1) / 2 → n 5: 2, 7: 3, 3: 1, odd n generally (n − 1)/2; even n (6): 2.5 per side. Chosen m = supported m closest to the prior; tie → lower BIC. Report data-only m (BIC_min) and chosen m. Surfels → component of highest responsibility; leaflets ordered by mean s.
5. **Pairs.** Pair k = k-th leaflet from the node on each side. Unequal sides: the shorter side is aligned to the longer by the order-preserving deletion that minimises Σ |Δ mean s|; the unmatched leaflet is a single-sided pair (missing side recorded).
6. **Per leaflet:** own area = Σ area (b); centroid; mean s; base = centroid of its 5 % surfels nearest the rachis curve, base s = their median s; plane by PCA of the surfels; long axis = first PCA axis, oriented from base to centroid; length / width = 1st–99th percentile range along axes 1 / 2; normal = surfel normals flipped to agree with the PCA normal (oriented +z), averaged within **2 mm** (same leaflet), then area-weighted mean; elevation / azimuth of the long axis; outline mesh = surfels thinned to one per **1 mm** cell in the leaflet plane (cell centroid in 3D), Delaunay in the plane, triangles with circumradius ≤ **2 mm** (α); no triangle across leaflets.
7. **Conservation.** Σ own leaflet area + rachis area = Σ area (b) of the leaf's labelled surfels (± 1e-9 mm²). The spec compound area (`area_mm2`) is not changed; any difference between it and the labelled-surfel sum is reported. In the tree each leaflet's `area_mm2` = own area + its area-proportional share of the rachis-surfel area **scaled so that Σ leaflets = spec `area_mm2`** (keeps the engine compound area = `_02` = B-model); own area, rachis share and scale are attributes.
