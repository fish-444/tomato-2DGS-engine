## 2. Light quantities per day
* Canopy light (%) = Σ_plants absorbed by leaflets ÷ emitted (1200 µmol s⁻¹) × 100.
* Neighbour shading of plant i = 1 − absorbed_i(canopy) ÷ absorbed_i(alone at its slot, same lamp, same ray count and seed); the alone value needs one extra light pass per plant: every **K = 5** days (user 1–10), held between updates; update days marked. Method: the other plants' organs are excluded from the light pass (implementation decides how; check: the alone pass of a 1-plant scene equals its canopy pass exactly). Run-time cost reported.
* Mean neighbour shading = mean over plants. Index = canopy light ÷ mean neighbour shading; shown only with its two parts; "—" when mean shading < 0.5 % (the ratio blows up as shading → 0).
